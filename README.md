# game-dev

Small browser games and toys.

## [Starfall](starfall/): 3D space strategy

A real-time, touch-first strategy game played across star systems in 3D space.
Send fleets to capture systems from AI rivals and spend ships on factory
upgrades. See [`starfall/README.md`](starfall/README.md).

## [Perihelion](perihelion/): one solar system, few ships

A slow strategy game inside a single solar system, in the spirit of hard
science fiction. Worlds orbit, ships flip and burn to intercept where their
target will be, and every ship counts. See
[`perihelion/README.md`](perihelion/README.md).

## [Covewind](covewind/) — free-flight toy

A no-objective arcade flying sandbox over a painted, cel-shaded Adriatic
archipelago: loops and rolls, tunnels and a blue grotto, lakes to land on.
Procedural world, procedural audio, no art assets. See
[`covewind/README.md`](covewind/README.md) to run it.

## [Dustline](dustline/): toy rally car in a canyon

A no-goal driving toy: a long-travel rally buggy loose in a red-rock desert
canyon packed with loops, kickers, a wall-of-death drum, a road spiralling up
a mesa to a ski jump, an elevated skyway, a canyon gap and a bowl-shaped rim
that always sends you back. Flip and spin in the air. See
[`dustline/README.md`](dustline/README.md).

## [Bellbrook](bellbrook/): toy fire engine in a little town

A no-lose toy for small children who love fire engines. Drive round a cute
town of houses, shops, a farm, a park and a seafront; when the bell rings,
follow the arrows, spray the fire until it's out, and everyone cheers.
Sometimes it's a kitten up a tree, so up goes the ladder. Siren, horn, blue
lights, people who wave, cars that pull over. See
[`bellbrook/README.md`](bellbrook/README.md).

## [Shellsong](shellsong/): a mermaid princess under the sea

Charlotte's game. Choose your mermaid's hair, skin, shell top, tail, crown
and throne colours, then swim: glowing grottos, sunken shipwrecks, twelve
treasures to find, rocks to sit on at the surface with ships and seagulls,
fish, turtles, stingrays, whales, dolphins to ride, and the underwater
palace where the King lives (and your own shell throne). See
[`shellsong/README.md`](shellsong/README.md).

All the games publish to GitHub Pages from `main`, with a landing page at the root
(`site/`) and each game in its own folder.
